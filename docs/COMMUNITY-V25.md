# v2.5 — Curated Instagram posts

Three user-supplied screenshots replace the homepage community placeholders. Each is trimmed at its outside edges only and encoded as lossless WebP, preserving all original content, lettering, faces and Instagram media icons. The cards use their natural 3:4 portrait ratio to avoid cutting off lettering. Each full card opens its corresponding original post in a new tab. No external embeds, scripts, APIs or database changes. Profile and founder Instagram links are unchanged. Confirm publication permissions as appropriate.

Source mapping and crop boxes are in asset-manifest.json.
